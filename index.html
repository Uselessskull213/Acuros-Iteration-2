Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025 · Last updated: March 2025

Acuros Health Inc. ("Acuros") is committed to protecting your personal health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including account registration details, health queries entered into the AI Assistant, medical documents uploaded for translation, appointment notes, and medication information. We also collect technical information automatically solely for security and service improvement.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, power our AI Assistant, maintain your Patient Portal, and comply with PIPEDA and applicable provincial legislation. We do not sell your health information or use it for advertising.

3. Legal Compliance

Acuros operates in compliance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where applicable, the Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) of Ontario.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on Canadian servers with AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, role-based access controls, and regular third-party security audits.

5. AI Data Handling

Queries entered into the AI Assistant are processed in real-time and not stored beyond your active session unless explicitly saved. Your queries are never used to train external AI models.

6. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal information. Contact: privacy@acuros.ca
