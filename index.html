05 — Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

Does Acuros integrate with my existing EMR?+
Acuros is designed as a complementary layer on top of any existing setup — no EMR integration or migration required. Your staff keep their current workflows and Acuros runs independently alongside them.
Is patient data stored on Canadian servers?+
Yes. All patient data is stored exclusively on Canadian servers and is subject to PIPEDA and PHIPA compliance requirements. Data is AES-256 encrypted at rest and in transit, and is never shared with third parties.
How long does setup take?+
Most clinics are fully onboarded the same day. After your demo call, we configure your clinic code, branding, and portal access — your patients can start enrolling within 24 hours.
Can patients book procedures and buy products online?+
Yes. Patients can access the OTC Shop for clinic-recommended over-the-counter products and use the Bookings page to schedule procedures and consultations directly through their portal.
What does the AI health assistant actually do?+
Acuros AI provides evidence-based health education: translating lab reports, explaining diagnoses, checking medication interactions, and helping patients prepare questions for their next appointment. It is educational only and explicitly not a substitute for professional medical advice.
